What Is Career?

Career is a project-based career development platform designed to help students bridge the skills gap between academia and industry.

The platform guides students through structured milestone projects and integrates career preparation tools such as CV and portfolio management.

Contribution

Research and UI Design

Problem Statement

Derived from student survey and interviews

68%
Students feel unsure about required industry skills
54%
Rely only on social media for career guidance
72%
Want structured, project-based learning

Research

To understand how students prepare for their careers and identify gaps in existing platforms, survey and interview research were conducted. The findings were synthesized through empathy mapping and persona development.

Emotional & Behavioral Insights

Research revealed recurring emotional and behavioral patterns:

  • Users feel overwhelmed by theory-heavy learning
  • They fear producing non-industry-ready projects
  • They need structured guidance and milestone clarity

User Persona

Anton Abimaya
Student
Description

Anton is a university student who has started to feel anxious about his career readiness. He realizes that academic grades alone are not enough, yet he is unsure which skills he should develop to stay relevant to industry expectations.

Goals
  • Build real, portfolio-ready projects
  • Understand industry-relevant skills through practical experience
  • Complete projects step-by-step with clear milestones
  • Enter the job market with a strong CV and portfolio
Pain points
  • Learns skills randomly without clear direction
  • Confused about where to start building a portfolio
  • Unsure whether his projects meet industry standards
  • Has multiple certificates but lacks tangible project experience

Competitor Analysis

Beyond user insights, a competitive analysis was conducted to understand existing solutions in the market.

01
  • Course-based learning
  • Certification-focused
  • No structured project-to-career pathway
02
  • Guided learning tracks
  • Limited portfolio integration
  • Focus on course completion

Solution

Structured Milestone Projects
Step-by-step milestone projects with structured tasks and clear progress tracking.
Portfolio Builder Integration
Provides a structured interface for users to curate and showcase their completed projects.
Career Readiness Tools
Includes a CV builder and career support features to assist users in preparing job applications.
Progress & Skill Tracking
Visual dashboard to monitor milestone completion and career readiness.

Reflection

This project explores how structured, milestone-based learning can bridge the gap between education and industry readiness.

Career demonstrates how guided projects and progress visibility can increase user confidence and clarity in career preparation.
