Who I Am

I'm an Informatics student at Universitas Gunadarma with a strong interest in UI/UX and Product Design, focused on building user-centered digital solutions for mobile-first experiences. I have hands-on experience designing user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes, with a solid understanding of design systems and scalable interfaces.

Through academic projects and national competitions, I've worked on digital products spanning education, health-tech, and public systems, always starting from research and validating ideas before translating them into interfaces that are both intuitive and measurable.

I'm especially interested in how data, usability testing, and systems thinking can shape better product decisions, and I enjoy collaborating closely with development teams to turn user needs into functional, real-world designs. Alongside design, I also build full-stack web applications end to end, from database and backend logic to the interface itself.
