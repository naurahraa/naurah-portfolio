01 — Overview

What Is This Project?

This is my Penulisan Ilmiah (PI), a mandatory supervised research course at Gunadarma University with its own final defense (sidang), typically taken from semester 6 onward: a full-stack web application built for Toko Azzam Jaya, a school uniform retailer in Jakarta that has operated for more than 30 years using entirely manual stock and transaction records.

The system gives the store owner real-time stock and sales management, gives customers an online catalog and ordering flow, and includes an AI chatbot that helps customers figure out the right uniform size before they buy.

Tech Stack

Laravel 13 PHP 8.3 MySQL 8.0 Bootstrap 5 RAG Chatbot
02 — The Problem

Derived from on-site observation and an interview with the store owner.

01

Stock and daily transactions were tracked manually, relying on memory and payment history, with no real-time way to know what's actually in stock.

02

Customers had no way to check product availability online, so they had to call or visit the store first, an inefficient process for both sides.

03

Parents often struggled to determine the correct uniform size for their child, previously relying entirely on the owner's decades of hands-on fitting experience.

03 — Key Features

What The System Does

01
Stock & Product Management

Admin can manage product variants (type, color, size) and track stock movement, with automatic alerts when stock is running low.

02
Online Catalog & Ordering

Customers can browse the catalog with search and filters by category and education level, then place orders online before visiting the store.

03
Digital Receipt

Completed orders generate a downloadable digital receipt (PNG), so customers have proof of purchase without a physical printout.

04
Sales & Stock Reports

Printable reports summarizing transactions and stock movement over a selected period, for the owner to review store performance.

04 — Highlight

The AI Sizing Chatbot

The standout feature of this project is a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot that acts as a virtual store assistant. Instead of guessing, customers can simply ask a question, and the chatbot answers using a real sizing guide grounded in the store owner's own fitting knowledge, not a generic AI guess.

Ukuran baju untuk kelas 3 SD?
Ada kemeja putih ukuran 15?
Seragam pramuka SMP apa saja?

A Closer Look

Reflection

I've built websites before for coursework and lab assignments, but this was the first one I carried through a complete, formally documented software development lifecycle, from requirements gathering to a tested, working product with the waterfall method.

Designing the chatbot's grounding logic, rather than letting it answer freely, taught me how important guardrails are when AI talks directly to real customers making purchasing decisions.
